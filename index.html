Questions

Before you buy

Do I need any software?

No. Every prompt is plain text you paste into any AI chat tool you already use — ChatGPT, Claude, or similar. The checklists and guides are PDFs you read and follow directly.

Is this specific to one industry or supply category?

No — the prompts work for any RFQ category. The markup reference table in Tier 2 and 3 covers lab equipment, IT, furniture, PPE, stationery, CCTV, food & beverage, and specialised items; you can edit it to your own categories.

Who actually wrote this?

Amanani (Pty) Ltd, a registered CSD supplier actively bidding and winning RFQs with University of Fort Hare, Walter Sisulu University and Buffalo City Metropolitan Municipality. Every step reflects a real disqualification risk or pricing mistake encountered running that business — not generic advice.
